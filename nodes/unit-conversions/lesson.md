# Unit conversions

A **conversion factor** is a fraction equal to 1, built from two equal quantities in different units. Since 1 km = 1000 m, both of these equal 1:

(1000 m / 1 km) and (1 km / 1000 m)

Multiplying by a conversion factor changes the units without changing the amount. Choose the version that cancels the unit you want to remove. This method is called **dimensional analysis**.

Common metric prefixes:

| Prefix | Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| kilo | k | 1000 |
| centi | c | 1/100 |
| milli | m | 1/1000 |

## Worked example: convert 2.5 km to centimeters

Go through meters, one step at a time.

2.5 km × (1000 m / 1 km) × (100 cm / 1 m) = **250 000 cm**

Kilometers cancel in the first step and meters cancel in the second, leaving centimeters.

## Common mistake

Using the conversion factor upside down. If the unwanted unit does not cancel, flip the fraction. Writing the units in every step makes this error easy to see.
