# Converting between mass and moles

Balances measure grams, but reactions happen in moles. Molar mass is the bridge between them.

- grams → moles: **divide** by molar mass
- moles → grams: **multiply** by molar mass

## Worked example: grams to moles

How many moles are in 36.0 g of H2O?

Molar mass of H2O = 18.015 g/mol

36.0 g ÷ 18.015 g/mol = **2.00 mol**

## Worked example: moles to grams

What is the mass of 0.250 mol of NaCl?

Molar mass of NaCl = 22.990 + 35.45 = 58.44 g/mol

0.250 mol × 58.44 g/mol = **14.6 g**

## Check yourself with units

If the units do not cancel to what you want, the operation is upside down. g ÷ (g/mol) leaves mol; mol × (g/mol) leaves g.
